# landform: Topographic Position Index-Based Landform Classification

Classifies a landscape into slope-position or landform classes on the
basis of the Topographic Position Index (TPI) and slope (Weiss 2001;
Jenness 2006).

## Details

Main functions:

- [`landform`](https://gianmarcoalberti.github.io/landform/reference/landform.md):
  Slope Position and Landform classifications, returned as an object
  with `print`, `summary` and `plot` methods;

- [`tpi`](https://gianmarcoalberti.github.io/landform/reference/tpi.md):
  the TPI surface itself, raw or standardised;

- [`class_layers`](https://gianmarcoalberti.github.io/landform/reference/class_layers.md):
  one presence/absence layer per class;

- [`malta_dtm`](https://gianmarcoalberti.github.io/landform/reference/malta_dtm.md):
  a sample digital terrain model.
